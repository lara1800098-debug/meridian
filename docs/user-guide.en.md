# Meridian — User guide

**Meridian finds new business early and keeps the pipeline honest.** It never sends anything for you, and every fact links to its source.

## Signing in
Use your work email and password, then enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. Sessions end after 30 minutes of inactivity.

## Executives & Directors
- **Cockpit (home):** the state of the business in five seconds.
  - Pipeline, weighted, realistic forecast vs target, coverage, Meridian-sourced pipeline, deals at risk, new leader-grade plays.
  - Click any number to see what is behind it.
- **Monday Brief:** waits for you at the start of your work week (Monday in the UAE, Sunday in KSA), in your language.
  - A 3-minute, one-page read.
  - **Export PDF** for the move.
- **Deal review:** the deals your weekly meeting should discuss, what changed since last week, and the questions to ask the owner. Export to PDF.
- **Forecast:**
  - Owner commit, weighted and Meridian realistic (the method is shown).
  - Coverage and concentration.
  - Funnel, velocity, win/loss.
  - Directors can set targets here.

## AEs & BDRs
- **Plays.** Each card shows:
  - **Why now** (facts with sources);
  - the recommended Concentrix offer and proof point;
  - an indicative value;
  - the scores with reasons;
  - risks;
  - ready Arabic and English outreach. Copy it and send it from your own mailbox.
- **Accept → deal.** One click creates the deal in your pipeline.
- **Dismiss.** Always pick a reason; it teaches Meridian what works.
- **Quick update** on any deal: stage, next step, date and one line, in under 60 seconds, including on your phone.
- **Health:** Meridian flags deals that have gone quiet, have no next step, keep slipping, miss stage criteria, or have news working against them. It also suggests one next best action.
- **Tender Reader:** upload an RFP (PDF/DOCX, Arabic or English). Meridian extracts the key terms with the original wording, gives a Go/No-Go checklist, and can put the deadlines on a deal.
- **Quick Sizer:** a rough FTE and seat range, and a deal value from approved rate cards. It is indicative only, never for pricing.
- **Access Map:** record who you know at which organisation. Meridian uses it to suggest the best path in. Only you, Directors, Executives and Admin can see your entries.
- **Add a signal:** paste a public link or upload a document. Meridian reads it, scores it and, if it is strong enough, creates a play.

## Language, currency, dates
- Switch **ع / EN** in the header.
- Choose the display currency (AED, SAR, USD, EGP, JOD, TRY).
- Tap **هـ** to add Hijri dates.
- Set your time zone and work week in **My settings**.
